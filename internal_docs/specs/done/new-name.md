# A new name for the app — done in 0.9.9: TreniDay (owner, 2026-10-12 / 10-13)

> Part of the project notes — the index is [internal_docs/README.md](../../README.md).

**Done (0.9.9, 2026-10-13):** the owner chose **TreniDay** and asked for the rename everywhere at once — the app, its ids, the site, the docs and the repository; the decision is in [decisions](../../decisions/README.md#the-app-is-called-treniday-owner-2026-10-13), the work in [CHANGELOG § 0.9.9](../../CHANGELOG.md). The store listings follow when they exist. A trademark filing is the owner's choice (not done).

**The wish:** while making the landing page the owner learned that the `caliday` domains are taken and asked to think of another name; then (2026-10-13): play with "training" and "day / daily" so that the name says *daily workouts*; Goro stays the mascot; choose the name first, then everything else.

**What was found about CaliDay (2026-10-12 / 10-13):**
- `caliday.com` is registered and parked for sale (Afternic); `caliday.app` is registered.
- **CALIDAY is filed as a US trademark in classes 41 / 43** (Celebree Holding, 2022 and again 2026-09-18), and in France in classes 3 / 5 / 16 (2026-09-17). Class 41 covers fitness training, so keeping the name would have risked a conflict in the US.

**The shortlist (2026-10-13).** Domains by the registries' RDAP (`.com` Verisign, `.app` Google, others through rdap.org; checked against `caliday`, which came out as known), the App Store through the iTunes Search API (US and DE), a web search:

| Name | Idea | .com / .app / .fit | Notes |
|------|------|--------------------|-------|
| **TreniDay** (chosen) | "treni" (Russian colloquial for training sessions; training, entrenar) + day; shaped like CaliDay | all free; also `.io .net .org .co .dev .de .ru` | no app or brand found; see below |
| Trainsday | "the day for training", read like Tuesday / Thursday | all free | "trains" are also railway trains |
| Exerday | exercise + day, written like "everyday" | all free | "exer" is less clear in German / Spanish |
| Repaday | "a rep a day" | all free | reads as "turnip" (репа) in Russian |
| Trainiday | CaliDay with Train for Cali | all free | too close to TrainerDay, a cycling app |
| TrainDaily, TrainingDay | — | taken | apps with these names exist; "Training Day" is also a film |
| Trainday, DayTrainer | — | `.com` taken | close to TrainerDay |

**TreniDay, checked in detail (2026-10-13):**
- No app named so in the App Store (US, DE, RU, ES, IT, GB) or on Google Play; for "treni" both stores show Italian train apps (Trenitalia, Trenord) — "treni" is Italian for trains.
- TMview (the EU offices, USPTO and others): nothing for "treniday"; the fuzzy search finds only TRENDAY (trend + day) — in Mexico in classes 9 and 41 (Expansion S.A.), in France 35 / 38 / 45, Finland 35, China 20, Japan 14 — and TRENADAY (India, class 5). The Mexican one is the closest; a lawyer would weigh it only for a Mexican launch. WIPO's Global Brand Database stopped at a bot check and was not searched.
- Free: the repository name, the GitHub user `treniday`, YouTube `@treniday`, the Telegram name looked free; Instagram and TikTok need a login and were not checked.
- To know: in English "treni" does not sound like "train", so the store title carries it ("TreniDay: Daily Workouts"); bodybuilders' slang "tren" means trenbolone — the capital D makes it read Treni·Day.

**Securing the name (the owner's choice):** a word mark "TreniDay" in classes 9 (the app) and 41 (fitness training): the DPMA (Germany, online €290 for up to three classes) or the EUIPO (the whole EU, online €850 for one class, +€50 for the second); within six months a filing abroad (WIPO Madrid, e.g. the US) keeps the first date. The App Store name is held by the app record in App Store Connect once the developer account exists.

**What the rename touched:** see CHANGELOG § 0.9.9. Not touched: the git history, the local folder of the checkout, and the two changelog lines about the old name itself.
