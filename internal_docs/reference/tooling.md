## Lottie Animation Tooling

Exercise animations are flat "paper-doll" Lottie files (one shape layer per body part, no parenting, 400×400, 12 fps). The Flex, supplementary, Posture and Neck sets (22 files incl. three reused) are generated, not hand-drawn; the older branches come from the original designer files:

- `tools/lottie/goro_rig.py` — Goro rig: forward/inverse kinematics → keyframed Lottie JSON. Same part sizes, colours and layer order as the existing files.
- `tools/lottie/gen_flex.py`, `tools/lottie/gen_supp.py`, `tools/lottie/gen_posture.py`, `tools/lottie/gen_neck.py`, `tools/lottie/gen_cooldown.py`, `tools/lottie/gen_pull.py`, `tools/lottie/gen_push.py` — one function per animation (a list of key poses); `python3 tools/lottie/gen_flex.py [--out DIR] [name ...]` rewrites `assets/animations/flex_*.json`, `gen_supp.py` the `supp_*.json` set, `gen_posture.py` the generated `posture_*.json` files (dead bug and kneeling lunge reuse `supp_dead_bug.json` / `flex_s1_hip_flexor_stretch.json`; pigeon has none), `gen_neck.py` the `neck_*` files and `warmup_neck_rolls.json`, `gen_cooldown.py` `cooldown_cat_cow.json`, `gen_pull.py` the redrawn `pull_s2..s6` and `warmup_dead_hang.json`, `gen_push.py` the redrawn `push_s4..s7` (the oldest designer files were reviewed in 2026-10; the redrawn ones replace them under the same names). `enliven.py` and `patch_old.py` change designer files **in place** (the first keyframe of each layer is the pose, the patches set absolute values, so a repeated run gives the same file; the pristine originals are in git history before the commit "review of the oldest designer animations, part 2"). Poses that depend on pinned contacts (planted feet) are generated as dense linear keys every 2 frames (`sampled()` in `gen_posture.py`) so IK keeps them planted.
- `tools/lottie/topview.py` — a second, top-down rig for poses that fail in profile: Goro seen from above lying on his back, drawn like the front-view asset (`warmup_wrist_circles`: same head/torso/blue sleeves, scaled by 0.6), parts placed by their end points. Used by `supp_oblique_crunch`.
- `tools/lottie/frontview.py` — a third rig, for standing poses seen from the front: same head/torso/blue sleeves as `topview.py` (scale 0.6, so the figure has the profile size: hips `(200, 288)`, floor `y = 376`), standing legs with lighter thighs and knee caps. `figure(hips, lean, head, sh_l/sh_r, arm_l/arm_r, leg_l/leg_r, torso_scale)` solves one frame from joint positions (arms by two-bone IK, legs by explicit knee/ankle points, so a knee raised towards the camera is a foreshortened thigh plus a bigger knee cap); `Animation(name, frames, pose_fn)` samples it every 2 frames. Layers are named by screen side (`l` = left of the picture). Optional round shoulder caps (`ORDER_CAPS`) make shrugs and shoulder circles readable, `head_scale` squashes the head (chin tucked), static props come from `goro_rig.bar` / `door_post` (door frame, wall). Used by `posture_s4_hip_march`, four of the Neck animations (rolls, tilts, shoulder circles, doorway stretch) and the front-view pull-ups. **Seated (0.8.20):** `SEAT_HIPS` (torso bottom at y 352) with `cross_legs()` / `butterfly_legs(knees_up)` / `straddle_legs(width)` for the leg layout and `order=ORDER_SEATED` (shins over knees over thighs, arms over everything); leg dicts take `foot_rot` / `foot_scale` / `foot_off`; `fold_toward(k)` folds the torso towards the camera (it foreshortens, the head comes down in front of it and cross-fades to the `crown` layer, the top of the head — layers now take a per-frame opacity `o`); `hand_rot` turns the fists (palms together). `view='back'` with `ORDER_BACK` draws Goro from behind (no face, the headband all the way round, spine and shoulder blades) for hands behind the back. `swing_arm(start, end, u, turn, shoulder=None, uturn=None)` moves an arm by its joints — the forearm rotates about the elbow, optionally the upper arm about the shoulder — instead of sliding the wrist past the elbow (that made `check_anim` jumps). Used by `gen_evening.py`. The standing outputs are byte-identical to before.
- `tools/lottie/gen_evening.py` — the 19 Evening Stretch animations: seated front views (`frontview.py`), side views (`goro_rig.py`: lying on the back via `supine()`, on the stomach and kneeling via `spine_at()` — the three-part spine anchored at the pelvis or the hip joint), top views (`topview.py`: `TopViewAnimation(name, frames, pose_fn, order, shapes_fn=None)` now takes any pose function and, for Goro face down, other shapes; the oblique crunch is unchanged) — the supine twist, the figure four and the frog (face down, the back seen). Legs folded over the body are drawn lighter (`LEG_LIGHT` through `Spec(tint=)`). `with_segment()` adds a strap layer between two points per frame (the towel); `lerp_angles()` interpolates joint angles the short way round. `Animation(style='paper')` draws dark arms and fists like the original files, `arm_len` stretches the arms (85 px), `props_index` puts a prop between figure layers (the pull-up bar sits over the head but under the fists) and `floor=False` omits the floor.
- `tools/lottie/gen_morning.py` — the Morning Routine animations (0.9.0, in progress; every pose standing). Front views on `frontview.py` with two helpers: `compose(legs, upper)` joins the legs of one `figure` to the upper body of another, so the torso can be placed apart from the hip joints (`hips_for(c, lean)` gives the `hips` that put the torso centre at `c`) — the windmill folds towards the camera with the shoulder line turning vertical; `with_back_copies(pose_fn, names, front)` adds `<name>@b` copies of layers lower in the order and cross-fades them, for a limb that passes in front of the body on one side and behind it on the other (the skater's back foot, the twist's arm behind the back). `frontview.Animation(shapes_fn=...)` takes extra or replaced shapes (`None` = the default): the twist draws the torso without its chest patch and the patch as a layer of its own that slides towards the side the torso turns to. Front-view helpers: `straight(sh, a, length)` (a straight arm by its world angle — fast sweeps driven by IK targets bent and flipped the elbow), `bent_leg` (IK knee out over the toes for lunges), `lerp_keys`, `clasped`; `HEAD_OVER_HANDS` draws the head over the fists (hands behind the head); the shake-out is sampled every frame (`step=1`: a 4-frame shake aliases at the usual 2-frame keys). Side views on `goro_rig.py`: `placed(pose, limb, at)` moves a pose so a joint lands on a point; `plank(shoulder)` / `PLANK_BR` (62.5°: the shoulders 80 px over the hands, the feet on the floor); the dog and the inchworm move the hips on an arc of leg length round the planted feet so the legs stay straight; `floor_arm` plants a hand with the elbow always on the same side of the arm (no IK flips); near legs over the body are drawn in Evening Stretch's `LEG_LIGHT`; the roll-down uses the three-part spine anchored at the hip joint.
- `tools/lottie/gen_yoga.py` — the Yoga animations (0.9.1, all 18 in the app). Side views are mostly `_key` poses run by `_flow(name, keys)` (the sun salutations, chair, Warrior I; `_sun_states()` holds the shared poses); `_on_far_leg` stands a pose on the far leg (Warrior III, dancer); front views use `wide_legs` and `_tilted` (triangle, side angle: an upper arm rising through the side, a lower elbow bent outwards). **Reach limit:** Goro's arm (80 px) is shorter than hip-to-shoulder (89 px), so a hand holds a foot or a heel only with the spine arched or the heel close above the hips (camel: hands on the back; dancer and bow searched numerically). Lying on the back, a spine bend that lifts the hips is **negative**. Front views: the half moon (`figure(lean=-80)`: the torso level in the picture plane, the standing hip joint kept over the ankle) and the eagle (the wrapping shin and foot get `@b` copies under the standing shin, cross-faded once across). Side views on the three-part spine: `body(hip, chest, s1, s2)` places a spine pose by the near hip joint; the camel (kneeling on tucked toes, the hands stay on the lower back: Goro's arms reach the heels only with the chest level behind him, which read as a bow), the wheel (anchored at the shoulder: the hips rise first, then the chest), and sun salutation A — key poses as parameter dicts (`_key`: hip, chest, spine, head, arms relative to the chest, `w` = how planted the hands are, the ankles and foot transforms), interpolated per frame (`_blend`, a lifted arc for a foot that moves) and solved by `_build` (feet and hands re-planted by IK, a leg behind the hip bends towards the floor).
- `tools/lottie/gen_extra.py` — the 22 animations of the branches outside the courses (0.9.5). Top views (`topview.py`, Goro face down with the back seen, as the frog): Y-T-W and reverse snow angels — an arm lifted off the floor is drawn a little bigger (closer to the camera). Back view (`frontview.py`, `view='back'`, two `figure`s joined by `compose`): the fire hydrant on all fours — the back foreshortened, the head beyond it, the arms down at the far end; a thigh pointing down is short, lifted out to the side it shows its full length (the top view first tried hid that Goro is on all fours). Side views (`goro_rig.py`): the superman and swimmers on the prone three-part spine (arms reaching ahead past the head, drawn over it); donkey kicks on the bird-dog base; the single-leg bridge on the Yoga bridge; the hip thrust with the upper back on a sofa edge (a *low* sofa: with Goro's 84 px legs a knee-high one leaves no room for a horizontal thigh); planks via `gen_morning.plank`; the door-frame row with the feet just past the post (`door_post`) — with them at the post Goro's arms allowed only ~18° of lean; chair props (`chair_props`) behind the dips — the torso's back stays in front of the seat's front edge below the seat top (`_seat_gap`) — and under the feet of the elevated pike; the pike push-ups (on the floor and with the feet on a chair) are drawn with other proportions (owner, 2026-10-11: the exercise must read, the proportions may change) — the torso 0.72 of its length (`sc_len`, below), the arms 1.15, the legs 1.5 / 1.2 (`_scaled_limb`: IK with scaled lengths, then the `sc_*` segment scales), the hips where the circles round the feet and round the shoulders meet (`_meet`), so the V has the hips on top and the head between the arms; the straight-leg dips solve the torso angle numerically so the hips stay on an arc round the heels; the burpee, half burpee and squat jumps are chains of key poses as the sun salutation (`_bkey` / `_bbuild` / `_bblend`, hands at `BURPEE_HANDS`, closer to the feet than the yoga ones). A leg in line with the body has the angle `br` itself (the torso's local down), not `br + 180`. The skater jumps are the Morning speed skater with a flight between the feet. `check_anim.py` reports the fast moves of the cardio jumps (the feet jumping back and in, the arms swinging up): that is the jump, not a broken pose.
- `tools/lottie/thumb_crops.py` — the square of each animation for its thumbnail (`lib/data/static/animation_crops.dart`); run after adding or redrawing an animation (§ Exercise animations wherever an exercise is named).
- `tools/lottie/frame_sheet.py` — still frames of any generated file side by side as inline SVG (`--count`, `--at`, `--crop` to zoom), no dependencies; open it through the `lottie-sheets` server in `.claude/launch.json` (serves `build/` on port 8098). `build_preview.py` needs `PYTHONUTF8=1` on Windows.
- `tools/lottie/check_anim.py` — jump / loop-seam checker (`python3 tools/lottie/check_anim.py NAME|FILE ...`): reports any layer that moves more than 22 px or turns more than 30° between consecutive frames, and any layer whose last frame differs from its first. Run it on every generated file.
- `tools/lottie/build_preview.py` (+ `preview_template.html`) — builds a self-contained preview page that plays the animations (pause, speed 0.5–1.5×, per-frame scrubber, key-frame strip, thumbnail/screen/large sizes). `python3 tools/lottie/build_preview.py [--preset flex|supp|posture|neck|cooldown|pull|push|refresh|evening|morning|yoga] [--fragment] [--out FILE] [--dir DRAFTS] [name ...]` writes `build/lottie_preview.html` (git-ignored); card copy lives in the `INFO` dict, page titles in `PRESETS`.
- Conventions: Goro faces right; segment angle `a` points along `(-sin a, cos a)` (0 = down, 180 = up, clockwise-positive); feet/hands are planted with IK targets, free limbs use FK angles; the floor line is `y = 376` and objects nearer the camera may dip into the floor strip below it and segments can be foreshortened (`sc_*`); both are supported but currently unused.
- `tools/lottie/pushup.py` — a fourth rig: push-ups in the exact style of the original `push_s1..s3` (their shapes and colours are copied from `push_s3_full_pushup.json`: a 130 × 50 torso, 24 / 20 px arms, a richer head). A pose is ankle, shoulder, hands and head; the body is one straight line (shin, thigh, torso), arms by two-bone IK with optional foreshortening (`uarm_sc`), straight foreshortened arms (`straight`), explicit elbows (`elbows`) and a vertical flip for an inverted body (`flip`). Used by `push_s4..s7` (the hands are shown by a top-down inset built with `rbar` / `oval`; the handstand is back to the wall).
- `tools/lottie/enliven.py` / `tools/lottie/patch_old.py` — in-place work on designer files that read well: a sway or rock of every figure layer about a contact point (hands, standing foot), and absolute-value patches (a head position, an arm colour). `Spec(tint={layer: colour})` in `goro_rig.py` recolours a body part (the stretched leg of `cooldown_quad_stretch`).
- Props: `Spec(props=lambda frames: [...])` adds static layers behind the figure (`bar`, `door_post` in the colours of the original wall / post). `hold_ramp` (eased piecewise curve) and `sampled` (dense linear keys from a pose function) live in `goro_rig.py` too.
- Three-part spine (cat-cow): spec `order` uses `sp_chest` / `sp_mid` / `sp_pelvis` instead of `body`; pose key `spine = (s1, s2)` bends the mid part about `SPINE_H1` and the pelvis about `SPINE_H2` (cumulative, degrees). `gen_cooldown.py` drives it with one total-bend parameter and solves the chord slope so the shoulders keep the height of the straight arms.
- Split torso (lying poses that need a lumbar arch): spec `order` uses `torso_u` + `pelvis` instead of `body`; pose key `pel` rotates the pelvis about the waist and `joint_of` moves the hip joints with it. Lying on the back, lower the hip joints (`hip_n=-6, hip_f=-12`) or the knees stay almost flat (the torso is 56 px thick).
- Limb tricks in the rig: `sh_n`/`sh_f` move the two shoulders along the torso (fake a torso twist), `sc_torso` widens the torso as it turns to the viewer, and an `order` entry like `uarm_r@b` plus `fade` crossfades a second copy of a limb so it can pass from in front of the torso to behind it (Russian twists). Poses built from IK targets can end up 360° apart from their neighbours; `align()` picks the short way round.
- `sc_len` (pose key, default 1) scales the length of the `body` torso: the shoulders and hip joints move with it, the head offset is the caller's (`(7, -83 + 50 * (1 - sc_len))`). At 1 every existing file regenerates byte for byte (checked on 77 files).
- A **fully folded** arm or leg (target closer than `|l1 - l2|`) has one IK solution only: the elbow / knee points at the target and `bend` changes nothing. Aim the target off the joint in the direction the elbow should point (the shoulder taps: just behind and below the shoulder, so the elbow drops back towards the feet).
- IK knee/elbow direction is chosen with a `bend` vector: knees bend forward/up for planted legs (`(1, -1)`, `(0, -1)`), but a leg stretched behind the body or kneeling must bend towards the floor (`(0, 1)`), otherwise the knee hyperextends.
- Quirk kept for consistency: the forearm is mounted upside-down (elbow = +y end, fist = −y end), so its layer rotation is `angle + 180`.

## Characters

Goro's poses and locked pose (`tools/characters/gen_goro.py`), the hosts (`gen_hosts.py`) and Skala (`gen_skala.py`) are generated SVGs — edit the scripts, not the files (details: [screens.md § Mascot Goro](screens.md#mascot-goro), `design-system/caliday/BRAND.md`). `tools/characters/export_widget_art.py` renders the faces, poses and flex to PNG for the native widgets ([platform.md § Home-screen widgets](platform.md#home-screen-widgets-097)).

## Sounds

`tools/sounds/gen_sounds.py` synthesises the 21 workout cues, `tools/sounds/build_preview.py` builds a page to listen to them ([workout.md § SoundService](workout.md)).

## A local Flutter

There is no Flutter on the owner's Mac; a session clones the SDK at the CI version (`FLUTTER_VERSION` in `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, 3.47.7) into its scratchpad with its own `PUB_CACHE`. Android builds need JDK 17 / 21 (see platform.md § Android Widget), iOS builds CocoaPods.
