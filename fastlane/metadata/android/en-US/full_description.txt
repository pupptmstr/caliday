Home workouts that feel like a game. Goro the gorilla shows every move, counts every hold and cheers you on — from your first wall push-up to a free handstand.

TreniDay is free, with no ads, no account and no tracking. Everything you do stays on your phone.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pick a course: Calisthenics, Healthy Body, Yoga, Morning Routine or Evening Stretch — or put your own course together from any exercises.
• Train without touching the screen: holds start by themselves after a get-ready countdown, sides switch on their own, and a sound tells you what comes next.
• Level up: more reps, then more sets, then shorter rests — then a challenge, and the branch moves on to the next stage.

FIVE COURSES, FIVE HOSTS
• Calisthenics with Goro the gorilla — push, pull, core, legs, balance and flexibility, from basic to advanced.
• Healthy Body with Raffi the giraffe — posture, neck and flexibility for people who sit a lot. Joint-friendly.
• Yoga with Miso the cat — standing poses, balance on one leg, backbends, sun salutations and arm balances.
• Morning Routine with Aurora the lark — spine, joints, arms and a little energy: standing, quietly, no jumps.
• Evening Stretch with Luna the owl — a calm stretch before sleep: slowly, on the floor, ending lying down.
• Your own course — pick branches from any course or line up your own exercises; the app works out the reps, sets and rests for you.

EVERY WORKOUT COUNTS
• Strength Points (SP) for every set, with a bonus for the first workout of the day and for finishing everything.
• Streaks with freezes: every week of streak earns a freeze that saves it on a day you miss.
• Six ranks, from Beginner to Legend.
• 45 achievements — and one of them is a secret.
• Challenges judged by Skala the bull: meet his norm and the branch moves on.

MADE FOR REAL LIFE
• Short, Standard or Full: choose how much to do. Before you start, TreniDay shows an honest estimate of today's plan, learned from your own pace.
• Sound cues over your music: get ready, go, switch sides, rest is over.
• Widgets with Goro on the home screen: your streak, rank and today's plan.
• Health Connect: save your workouts with an estimate of the calories, if you want to.
• Gentle reminders: a morning reminder, an evening nudge and a warning when your streak is at risk — each one can be turned off.
• Over 150 exercises, each one animated, in a library you can search.
• Almost no equipment: a floor, a wall and sometimes a chair. A pull-up bar opens the Pull branch; without one, your workouts are planned around it.
• English, Russian, German and Spanish. A dark theme.

YOUR PROGRESS STAYS WITH YOU
TreniDay has no server and no account. Your workouts, progress and settings are stored on your device and nowhere else. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
